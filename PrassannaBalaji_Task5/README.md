# Task 5 - Social Engineering Attacks Research Report

## Internship

Oasis Infobyte Security Analyst Internship

## Objective

The purpose of this task is to research common social engineering attacks and explain how human behavior can be manipulated to compromise accounts, devices, and organizational systems. The report focuses on attack methods, psychological triggers, real-world incidents, business impact, and practical prevention strategies.

## Threats Covered

- Phishing
- Spear Phishing
- Whaling
- Vishing
- Smishing
- Pretexting
- Baiting
- Quid Pro Quo

## Project Files

| File | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `social_engineering_report.md` | Complete Task 5 research report covering social engineering attack types, case studies, mitigations, comparison table, training checklist, and references |

## Research Methodology

The research uses credible cybersecurity sources such as CISA, NIST, MITRE ATT&CK, SANS Institute references, official organization reports, and reputable cybersecurity publications. The information is paraphrased in original wording and references are provided for technical guidance, incident details, and security recommendations.

## Key Learning Outcomes

- Understanding human-focused attacks and why attackers target trust, urgency, fear, curiosity, helpfulness, and authority.
- Recognizing phishing techniques across email, phone calls, text messages, and targeted executive attacks.
- Understanding how attackers build believable pretexts to bypass normal verification.
- Learning why identity verification is important for help desk, vendor, and financial requests.
- Handling unknown USB devices, suspicious downloads, and fake software offers safely.
- Building employee security awareness as part of a layered defense strategy.
